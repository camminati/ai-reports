The Federal Reserve note (Apr 2026) compares three US surveys: the Census BTOS counts each firm once (about 18% at end-2025); the Atlanta Fed Survey of Business Uncertainty weights by employment (about 78% in Nov 2025), because large firms employ most people. Eurostat 2025: 19.95% of EU enterprises with 10+ employees. Bitkom 2026: 57% of German companies with 20+ employees (603 companies, phone survey, self-assessment). McKinsey 2026 (PDF, Exhibit 1): 89% of respondents report regular use in at least one function (88% in 2025, the figure the Stanford AI Index 2026 reports); 1,719 respondents in 97 countries, weighted by each country's share of global GDP. The rows are from different dates (Nov 2025 to summer 2026) and should not be read as one time series.
