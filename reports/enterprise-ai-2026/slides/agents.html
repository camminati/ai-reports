Agentic AI is a subset of enterprise AI: software that plans and acts across several steps. Gartner (Hype Cycle for Agentic AI, Aug 2026, citing its CIO survey): 17% have deployed agents, more than 60% expect to within two years; agentic AI sits at the Peak of Inflated Expectations. Deloitte (Tech Trends / Emerging Technology Trends 2025): 30% exploring, 38% piloting, 14% with solutions ready to deploy, 11% actively using in production. McKinsey 2026: 40% of organizations above $1bn revenue are scaling agents (27% before), against 22% of smaller ones, flat. Bitkom Sep 2026: 11% deploy, 29% plan, 31% looking into it (secondary report; the base is worded ambiguously). KPMG Q1 2026: 54% actively deploying agents. The bars use different scales of definition and different samples; they are shown together to make the wording effect visible, not as one series.
