Eurostat 2025: among enterprises that had considered AI but did not use it, the top reasons were lack of expertise (70.89%), unclear legal consequences (52.52%) and data protection concerns (48.83%). Bitkom Sep 2026 (via secondary reports, not checked against the full study): among AI users, 51% cite infrastructure such as cloud and compute as a major cost factor, 50% data preparation, 41% integration into existing systems. Bitkom's 2025 report separately lists legal uncertainty, missing know-how and staff shortages as barriers, consistent with Eurostat. European Commission (Stepup Startups) agentic AI report: organizations often delay agentic pilots over compliance, reputational risk and lack of mature reference cases. These are stated reasons, not measured causes.
