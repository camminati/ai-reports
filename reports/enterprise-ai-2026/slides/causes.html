Gartner article "Why 50% of GenAI Projects Fail" (Arun Chandrasekaran, 26 Jan 2026): at least 50% of generative AI projects were abandoned after proof of concept by the end of last year due to poor data quality, inadequate risk controls, escalating costs or unclear business value. Five failure points: lack of business value; data is not ready; escalating total cost of ownership; responsible AI treated as an afterthought; poor change management. Gartner claims organizations that master these fundamentals turn pilots into production at twice the rate of those that do not. Gartner press release 17 Aug 2026: AI inference costs per agentic workflow will increase more than fivefold through 2028. These are one analyst firm's conclusions from client work; the underlying data are not public. RAND (2024) reached a similar overall picture from interviews with data scientists and engineers, and cites estimates that more than 80% of AI projects fail, twice the rate of IT projects without AI; that 80% is described there as an estimate, not a measured rate.
