Bitkom KI-Studie: 2025 wave (published Feb 2026, 604 companies, fieldwork weeks 27-32 of 2025) 36% use AI, double the 20% of 2024; the September 2026 wave (603 companies, weeks 28-33 of 2026) reports 57%. Share saying AI is no topic: 41% (2024), 17% (2025), 4% (2026). Bitkom itself notes these are self-assessments, not measured figures. Note the source of confusion: 17% in the KI-Studie is the not-a-topic share, not usage; a separate Bitkom survey (Digitalisierung der Wirtschaft 2026) reports 41% using AI versus 17% earlier, on a different questionnaire. The agent split (11% / 29% / 31%) and the cost drivers (51% / 50% / 41%) come from secondary reports of the September 2026 wave; the agent question base is worded ambiguously and we could not confirm either in the primary press text.
