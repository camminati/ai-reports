Eurostat, enterprises with 10 or more employees, reference year 2025: 19.95% use at least one AI technology (13.48% in 2024). By size: small 17%, medium 30.36%, large 55.03%. Leaders: Denmark 42.03%, Finland 37.82%, Sweden 35.04%. Lowest: Romania 5.21%, Poland 8.36%, Bulgaria 8.55%. The definition is broad (text mining, speech recognition, text and image generation, machine learning, AI-based robotic process automation). There is no separate agent category. Sample: about 157,000 of 1.53 million enterprises. Eurostat plans its next update for December 2026. The 2030 target and the 2026 values for cloud and analytics come from the European Commission Digital Decade material; the AI figure there is stated as nearly 20%, up 48% in 2025.
