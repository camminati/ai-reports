At the extremes: an AI “minister”, and outright bans

Adoption at the edge

Albania · “Diella”. Chatbot on the e-Albania portal since Jan 2025; named “Minister of State for AI” on 11 Sep 2025, for procurement. Humans must approve its recommendations.

Rama: tenders will be “100% free of corruption”. Opposition: “a propaganda fantasy”. Its agency AKSHI is investigated over rigged tenders (over €40m seized); Diella’s own build was not among them.

Romania · “Ion”. A mirror-shaped AI adviser launched 1 Mar 2023 by the prime minister, built by volunteers, to relay citizens’ proposals. Early trouble: image copyright, unanswered privacy questions.

Restriction at the other edge

Italy · ChatGPT. Blocked in Mar 2023, lifted within a month after changes. Fined €15m on 20 Dec 2024. The Court of Rome annulled the fine on 18 Mar 2026, reportedly because the Garante lacked competence, not on the merits.

DeepSeek, 2025. Australia and Taiwan banned it on government devices (Feb); Italy blocked the app (Jan); the Czech Republic barred public administration (Jul); Germany asked Apple and Google to remove it from their app stores (Jun); South Korea suspended downloads.

Common reason: data security, especially storage in China.

No AI system holds a seat in any parliament in the sources reviewed. Diella addressed Albania’s parliament on 18 Sep 2025, but as a cabinet “minister”, not a member. Ion is an adviser.

Albania: Diella launched 19 Jan 2025 as a text assistant on eAlbania, developed by the National Agency for Information Society (AKSHI) with Microsoft and OpenAI technology; a presidential decree of 11 Sep 2025 made it "Minister of State for Artificial Intelligence" (first AI system in a cabinet role); it was to take over four procurement stages (drafting terms, eligibility criteria, price limits, document validity), with human experts approving all recommendations. Its parliamentary speech was on 18 Sep 2025; opposition leader Gazment Bardhi called it "a propaganda fantasy". On 26 Oct 2025 the prime minister said Diella was "pregnant with 83 children" (apparently AI assistants for members of parliament; documentation unclear). By mid-2025 the system had helped issue about 36,000 documents and serve about 1,000 services, but the outputs came from the eAlbania backend. OCCRP reports prosecutors investigating rigged tenders at AKSHI, with over 40 million euros of assets seized; OCCRP says her development was not among the tenders involved; how the probe timing relates to Diella is unclear; no one has been indicted. A Balkan Insight reality check of 14 Aug 2026 was blocked to us, so we do not report what Diella has achieved since. Romania: Ion was unveiled by Prime Minister Nicolae Ciuca on 1 Mar 2023 (RFE/RL), built by volunteer academics and industry experts; issues: an image used without permission, privacy questions, satirical reception. Restrictions: Italy's data protection authority blocked ChatGPT in March 2023, lifted in under a month, fined OpenAI 15 million euros on 20 Dec 2024 (of which 9m for unlawful processing, 5.68m for non-compliance with 2023 measures, 0.32m for late breach notice); the Court of Rome annulled the decision on 18 Mar 2026 (Wilson Sonsini gives no reasoning; the European Law Blog reports a jurisdictional ground: the Garante lacked competence under the GDPR one-stop-shop rules, after the Irish regulator recognised OpenAI Ireland as its single establishment); OpenAI had called the fine disproportionate. DeepSeek restrictions per a Reuters factbox: Australia (government devices, Feb 2025), Taiwan (Feb 2025), Italy (app blocked, Jan 2025), Czech Republic (public administration, Jul 2025), Germany (asked Apple and Google to remove the app, Jun 2025), Netherlands (civil servants), South Korea (new downloads suspended).
