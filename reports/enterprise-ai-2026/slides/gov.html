OECD Digital Government Outlook 2026 (36 responding countries; Germany and the United States did not participate; 2025 data): 35 of 36 use AI in at least one government function; by function: internal processes 31, public services 27, policymaking 13, oversight 12; 64% use generative AI for at least one purpose, most often staff productivity (56%) and automated reporting (42%); all but three members have AI-in-government strategies. Guardrails: 14 of 36 require pre-deployment risk assessments, 12 have internal review committees, 11 do post-deployment audits, 11 have formal transparency standards, 6 maintain open algorithm registers; only 10 of 36 measure financial or non-financial impact, although half base adoption decisions on efficiency projections. Only 13 of 36 deploy their own accelerators (GPUs). A second index, the Public Sector AI Adoption Index 2026 from the Center for Data Innovation (survey of 3,335 public servants in ten countries including Germany, sponsored by Google), was consulted but not used for numbers because it is sponsor-funded.
