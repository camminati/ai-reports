Source: Federal Reserve FEDS Note "Monitoring AI Adoption in the US Economy" (3 Apr 2026). Left bars: Census Business Trends and Outlook Survey, new series (question widened in Nov 2025 to AI in any business function). Professional, scientific and technical services and finance are "about 33 and 30 percent"; information, real estate, wholesale and accommodation/food are averages of the four surveys leading up to year-end 2025. Right bars: Real-Time Population Survey, share of workers in each sector using generative AI at work; finance 63%, professional services 62%; information 70%, real estate 58%, wholesale 48%, accommodation and food 21%. The two measures are different things (firms versus individual workers) and use different scales of respondent. The Fed note also says the jump between old and new Census wording ranged from 47% (professional services) to 159% (manufacturing). We did not find a comparable, verified EU or German industry breakdown.
