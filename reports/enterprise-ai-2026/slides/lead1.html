Scorecard of prominent forecasts by AI-industry leaders; verdicts are our reading of the cited evidence. (1) Amodei at CFR, 10 Mar 2025: "we will be there in three to six months, where AI is writing 90 percent of the code. And then in twelve months, we may be in a world where AI is writing essentially all of the code." Anthropic spokesperson to Fortune (29 Jan 2026): company-wide 70-90%, about 90% for Claude Code itself. Google blog 22 Apr 2026: 75% of new code is AI-generated and approved by engineers, up from 50% last fall; Semafor notes such claims are hard to fact-check and "new code" is undefined. Microsoft said 20-30% in Apr 2025. (2) Amodei, Axios 28 May 2025: AI could wipe out half of entry-level white-collar jobs and spike unemployment to 10-20% in one to five years. BLS 4 Sep 2026: unemployment 4.1%, payrolls +162,000 in August. Stanford Digital Economy Lab update (12 Aug 2026): 22-25-year-olds in AI-exposed jobs are 19% below the expected employment level, driven by reduced hiring; the authors call these early descriptive indicators and write that they do not see widespread, economy-wide job displacement associated with AI; the metric differs from the earlier 13% regression estimate. McKinsey, The state of AI in 2026 (PDF, p. 25, Exhibit 16; 1,521 AI-using respondents): 14% report that AI contributed to an overall decline in workforce size in the past year, 8% an increase, 66% little or no change; a year earlier 32% had expected declines. 39% now expect decreases next year, 43% little or no change. Self-reported. NY Fed (1 Sep 2026, regional survey of service firms in New York and Northern New Jersey): 4% laid off workers in response to AI over six months. (3) Altman, "Reflections", Jan 2025: "in 2025, we may see the first AI agents join the workforce and materially change the output of companies". Deloitte Tech Trends 2026 via Reworked: 11% of organisations had agents in production. Karpathy, 26 Feb 2026: coding agents basically did not work before December and basically work since. (4) Zuckerberg on Joe Rogan (Jan 2025, via Fortune): probably in 2025 an AI that can effectively be a mid-level engineer; LlamaCon 29 Apr 2025: maybe half the development done by AI within a year. TechCrunch 2 Jul 2026: he told staff agent development had not accelerated as expected. (5) Altman on X, 29 Oct 2025: internal goal of an automated AI research intern by September 2026, a true automated researcher by March 2028, "we may totally fail". OpenAI says in Sep 2026 that it reached the intern goal; nobody outside OpenAI can check. Several quotes were read via summaries; re-check against source before quoting. Colour key (our reading of the evidence): green = came as predicted; yellow = not yet, but on track; red = missed or off track; grey = cannot be checked. Judgement calls: the jobs forecast is red because unemployment is 4.1% against a 10–20% claim, although the window runs to 2030; Zuckerberg is red because Meta itself said in Jul 2026 that agents progressed more slowly than hoped; the OpenAI research intern is grey because only OpenAI can check it.
