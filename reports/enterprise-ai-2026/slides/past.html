Three back-tests we could construct from public sources. (1) Gartner July 2024 predicted at least 30% of generative AI projects would be abandoned after proof of concept by end-2025; Gartner's Jan 2026 article says by the end of last year at least 50% were abandoned after proof of concept, citing poor data quality, inadequate risk controls, escalating costs or unclear business value. Both numbers are Gartner's own and its method is not public. (2) S&P Global Market Intelligence, Voice of the Enterprise 2025 (1,006 respondents, North America and Europe): generative AI adoption exceeded forecasts organizations made in 2024; the share abandoning most initiatives before production rose from 17% to 42%; on average 46% of projects are scrapped between proof of concept and broad adoption. (3) Fed note: about 9% of firms planned to use AI within six months at the start of 2025; by end of June the adoption rate was in line with expectations; over 20% expected to use AI in the first half of 2026. Caveat: Census widened its question in Nov 2025, so later comparisons need care. Colour key (our reading of the evidence): green = came as predicted; yellow = not yet, but on track; red = missed or off track; grey = cannot be checked. The S&P row is red because the plans understated actual adoption; the direction was right, the size was not.
