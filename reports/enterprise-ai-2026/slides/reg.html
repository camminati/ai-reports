The Digital Omnibus on AI: provisional political agreement on 6 May 2026, published in the Official Journal on 24 July 2026 and in force from 27 July 2026 (Lewis Silkin). New dates: Annex III stand-alone high-risk systems 2 December 2027 (previously 2 August 2026); Annex I systems embedded in regulated products 2 August 2028 (previously 2 August 2027). Unchanged: prohibited practices (Article 5, in force since 2 February 2025; a ban on non-consensual intimate imagery and child sexual abuse material was added, with a transitional period until 2 December 2026), general-purpose AI obligations (since 2 August 2025) and Article 50 transparency duties (2 August 2026, with a grace period to 2 December 2026 for machine-readable labelling of systems placed on the market before 2 August 2026). Other changes: AI literacy duty now shown by documentation; national sandboxes by 2 August 2027; simplified registration for self-assessed non-high-risk systems; support for small mid-caps. Maximum fine for prohibited practices: 35 million euros or 7% of total worldwide annual turnover, whichever is higher (AI Act Article 99(3), per artificialintelligenceact.eu; not re-checked against the Omnibus). IW Köln IW-Report 2025 (IW Future Panel wave 49, 1,038 firms, 16 Oct to 5 Dec 2024): 69% of firms want support in dealing with the AI Act; 66.3% of large firms and 35.6% of small firms use AI.
