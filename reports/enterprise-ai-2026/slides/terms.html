Summary

Terms for AI adoption and value

TermPlain meaning
AdoptionAny use of AI in a company, however small
Pilot / proof of conceptA small test of whether an idea works
ProductionLive use by real staff or customers, with support and cost
ScalingSpread beyond one team to the wider organization
EBITOperating profit before interest and tax; the test of real value
AI agentSoftware that plans and takes multi-step actions on its own
Total cost of ownershipAll-in cost over the life: build, run, support, govern
De-adoptionA company that used AI stops using it

Definitions are simplified for this deck; each source uses its own wording.

3

Glossary for readers outside technology. Adoption is deliberately loose: it counts any use. The gap between adoption, production, scaling and EBIT impact is the theme of the deck. Total cost of ownership includes running costs; for AI these include inference cost, the cost each time a model produces an answer.
