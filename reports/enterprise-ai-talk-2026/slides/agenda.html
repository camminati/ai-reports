[1 min] Say: this talk has three parts. First, four findings from the public data. Second, the most likely causes, as five patterns that connect choices to outcomes. Third, five actions, one for each pattern. Name the four findings once, so the audience can recognise them when they come back: (1) use is broad, value is narrow; (2) failure means stopped pilots; (3) no model forecasts adoption and failure together; (4) workflow redesign leads the factors. Be clear about one point early: nearly all numbers are survey answers and many come from firms that sell advice. The caveats slide comes near the end, and the companion report lists every source.
