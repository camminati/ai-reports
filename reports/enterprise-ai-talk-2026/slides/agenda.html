[1 min] Say: nearly everyone has started with AI, few can show the profit. The talk follows one line: what we see, why it happens, what to do. Left to right on this slide. What we see: four findings from the public data. Name them once, so the audience can recognise them when they come back: (1) use is broad, value is narrow (89% use AI, about 6% report a significant profit impact); (2) failure means stopped pilots (42% scrapped most initiatives in 2025); (3) no validated model forecasts adoption and failure together (none found); (4) workflow redesign leads the factors (high performers are about three times as likely to have redesigned workflows). Why: five patterns that connect choices to outcomes, each rated for the strength of its evidence. The rows line up: action 1 answers pattern 1, and so on. What to do: five actions, one for each pattern, then six numbers to track in your own company. Be clear about one point early: nearly all numbers are survey answers and many come from firms that sell advice. The caveats slide comes near the end, and the companion report lists every source.
