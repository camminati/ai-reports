[1 min] Agentic AI means software that plans and acts on its own across several steps. It is a subset of the topic, and a good example of why definitions matter. Five sources, five answers: Deloitte (2025) 11% have agents in production, 38% are piloting, 30% exploring. Bitkom (Sep 2026, a secondary report with an unclear base) 11% of German companies deploy agents, 29% plan to, 31% are looking into it. Gartner (CIO survey, 2026) 17% have deployed agents, and over 60% expect to within two years. McKinsey (2026) 40% of firms above $1bn revenue are scaling agents, up from 27%, while smaller firms are flat at 22%. KPMG (Q1 2026) 54% are actively deploying, a wording that includes early stages. Message for a decision maker: when someone quotes an agent adoption figure, ask what exactly was counted. Figures that ask for "in production" cluster at 11 to 17%. Full report: slide 11.
