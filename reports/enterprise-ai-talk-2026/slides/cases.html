[1.5 min] Four public reversals show what the cost looked like. In all four it was quality and liability, not the technology failing to run. Klarna (Sweden): in Feb 2024 it said its AI assistant did the work of 700 agents. In May 2025 the CEO told Bloomberg that AI had been cheaper but of lower quality, and the company started hiring humans again. Reported as partly reversed. Commonwealth Bank of Australia: in July 2025 it announced 45 call-centre job cuts after introducing a voice bot. On 21 Aug 2025 it reversed the cuts and apologised; the union had reported rising call volumes. McDonald's with IBM (US): voice ordering tested at about 100 restaurants since 2021. Ended in June 2024 after videos of wrong orders went viral. Air Canada: in Moffatt v Air Canada (2024 BCCRT 149, decision of 19 Feb 2024) a British Columbia tribunal held the airline responsible for wrong refund advice given by its website chatbot. Caution: these are selected public cases. No source counts how often such reversals happen, so do not read them as a rate. They illustrate patterns 2 and 3 later in the talk. Full report: slide 12.
