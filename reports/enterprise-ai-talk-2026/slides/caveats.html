[1 min] How far to trust these numbers. Say it plainly. Definitions differ: McKinsey and Stanford count regular use in at least one function; Eurostat counts any of a list of AI technologies; Bitkom is self-assessed; Census BTOS changed its question in Nov 2025. So absolute levels are not comparable across sources. Direction and gaps are more reliable than levels. Mostly self-reported: executives answering surveys. Bitkom itself says its figures are self-assessments. Interested parties: Gartner, McKinsey and BCG are commercial research houses and their methods are only partly public. AI leaders have an interest in bold forecasts. Other interested parties flagged in the report: CISPE (cloud providers' association), the Cloud Security Alliance study commissioned by Zenity, and company-reported case results. Thin evidence: MIT NANDA's widely quoted "95% of organisations see no measurable return" rests on an anonymised, self-reported sample and is not used as a headline here. Gaps: no verified EU industry split, and no public count of agentic-project cancellations. The next official data point is a Eurostat update planned for December 2026.
