[1.5 min] Finding 1 for Europe. Left: Eurostat, official statistics for 2025: 20.0% of EU enterprises with 10 or more employees use AI, up from 13.5% in 2024. Size decides: 17.0% of small firms, 30.4% of medium firms, 55.0% of large firms. Country range: Denmark 42.0% down to Romania 5.2%. The EU's 2030 target is 75% of enterprises using cloud, big data and/or AI taken together; in 2026 AI alone is at nearly 20%. Right: Germany, Bitkom phone survey of about 600 companies per wave, companies with 20 or more employees: 20% (2024), 36% (2025), 57% (2026). These are self-assessments, not measured use. The share saying AI is no topic for them fell from 41% to 17% to 4%. Do not set 20% against 57%: different surveys, different firm sizes and a different question. What they share is the direction and that bigger firms are further along. Full report: slides 8 and 9.
