[2 min] Finding 4: workflow redesign leads the factors. Among the factors that separate the roughly 6% high performers from everyone else in McKinsey's 2026 survey, the widest gap is whether the firm fundamentally redesigned individual workflows: nearly three-quarters of high performers, about one-quarter of others. That is the "nearly three times" on the slide. Other differences: high performers are 3.3 times as likely to intend to transform the business within three years, and they use AI in more business functions. Size matters, too. McKinsey: 40% of firms above $1bn revenue are scaling agents versus 22% of smaller firms, flat. Eurostat: 55% of large EU firms use AI versus 17% of small ones. Caution: this is a correlation in a self-reported survey. High performers may redesign workflows because they already see value. Say so, and note that it is still the strongest signal across the sources. A weaker signal we leave out of the slide: MIT NANDA, second-hand, suggests bought solutions succeed more often than internal builds. McKinsey also reports that 32% of AI-using respondents decided against buying at least one software product because coding agents could build it in-house. Full report: slide 30.
