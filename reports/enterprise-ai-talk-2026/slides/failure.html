[2 min] Finding 2: failure means stopped pilots. This is how failure shows up in the data. S&P Global Market Intelligence (Voice of the Enterprise, 2025): 42% of companies scrapped most of their AI initiatives, up from 17% a year earlier. On average 46% of projects were scrapped between proof of concept and broad adoption. Gartner (Jan 2026): at least 50% of generative AI projects were abandoned after proof of concept by the end of 2025. In July 2024 Gartner had forecast "30% or more". Gartner (Jun 2025) forecasts that over 40% of agentic AI projects will be canceled by the end of 2027. This one is a forecast, shown dashed. No source counts agent cancellations directly yet; related indicators point in the same direction. Definitions differ: "scrapped most initiatives" and "abandoned after proof of concept" are not the same count, and Gartner and S&P both sell research. Treat the numbers as the same signal from two sources, not as one measurement. Full report: slides 2, 20 and 21.
