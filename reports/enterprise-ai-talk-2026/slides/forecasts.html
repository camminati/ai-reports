[1.5 min] Finding 3, first half: what happened to forecasts. Traffic lights: green came as predicted, yellow not yet but on track, red missed or off track, grey cannot be checked. Row 1: Gartner forecast in July 2024 that 30% or more of GenAI projects would be abandoned after proof of concept by the end of 2025. In January 2026 Gartner reported at least 50%. The forecast came true and was too low. Row 2: companies' own plans, as surveyed in 2024 by S&P Global, did not anticipate what happened: adoption went beyond the plans, and the share of firms scrapping most initiatives rose from 17% to 42% in a year. Both moved against the plan. Row 3: stated short-term intent held up. In early 2025, 9% of US firms said they planned to adopt AI within six months (Census BTOS); by June 2025 adoption was in line with that. This is the only real back-test we found, and it covers six months. Takeaway: adoption forecasts held or were beaten; failure forecasts were too low. Gartner is both the source of the forecasts and of the checks, so independent confirmation would be better. Full report: slides 20 to 22.
