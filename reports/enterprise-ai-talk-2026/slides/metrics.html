[1.5 min] How to know whether the actions work: six numbers to track in your own company. They mirror the funnel in the public data, so a company can place itself against it. Use: share of staff or units using AI each week. The public range runs from 18% (US Census BTOS) to 89% (McKinsey), depending on the definition, so define it before you compare. Scale: share of use cases running beyond one team. McKinsey: 44% of AI users say AI is scaling across the enterprise. Value: use cases with a measured profit or cost effect. McKinsey: 37% report any EBIT impact, about 6% a significant one. Conversion: pilots that reach production. S&P Global: on average 46% of projects were scrapped between proof of concept and broad adoption. Gartner: at least 50% abandoned. Run cost: monthly cost per completed AI workflow. Gartner expects agentic inference cost per workflow to rise more than fivefold through 2028. Drop-out: teams that stop using AI after launch. The Markov model assumes 5% a year; in one US survey 14.5% of users expected to stop within six months. Last line: benchmark on "in production", not "deploying". Wording alone moves the agent figure from 11% to 54%. Full report: slide 34.
