[1.5 min] Finding 3 in one sentence: there is no validated model that forecasts adoption and failure together. S-curve (Bass) diffusion describes how use spreads to a fixed pool of eventual users. It has no drop-out, and we found no enterprise GenAI validation of it. The Gartner Hype Cycle is widely quoted, but Steinert and Leifer examined it and found Gartner's placement did not match data for three technologies. Treat it as a narrative, not a forecast. Gartner is also an interested party. A Markov chain with drop-out (Hojdik et al., J. Open Innovation, Sep 2026) lets firms move between non-user, planner, user and back out. It has the right structure, but was fitted to 816 Slovak firms at a single point in time, so it is untested over time. With an assumed 5% yearly drop-out, adoption levels off below full adoption. Stated plans versus outcomes is the only real back-test we found. It held, but only for six months. Practical consequence, which becomes an action later: count the people and teams that stop using AI, not just those who sign up. In one US survey 14.5% of users expected to stop within six months.
