[1.5 min] Finding 4, second half: what analysts name as causes. Gartner (Jan 2026) lists five reasons why generative AI projects get abandoned: unclear business value; data that is not ready; escalating total cost of ownership; responsible AI treated as an afterthought; and poor change management. Numbers behind them. Data: only 5% of firms say their data is ready to support AI initiatives (Dun and Bradstreet, n = 10,000, May 2026); only 7% say it is completely ready (Cloudera and HBR Analytic Services, n above 230, Oct 2025). Gartner found 63% lack, or are unsure they have, the right data practices. Cost: Gartner expects the run cost of one agentic workflow to rise more than fivefold through 2028 (Aug 2026), even though the price per token falls (a 280-fold drop between Nov 2022 and Oct 2024 for GPT-3.5-level quality, Stanford AI Index). A task routed to an agentic reasoning model costs at least five times a chatbot answer. McKinsey: one in five firms limit AI use because of operating costs. Gartner also says that firms that master these five fundamentals get twice as many pilots into production. Gartner is an interested party; the survey figures differ in sample and wording and are not one time series. Full report: slides 22 and 31.
